Numeric expressions accept decimal, binary and hexadecimal values, parentheses, unary minus, named references, + - * / ^, and math functions such as sqrt, exp, log, trigonometric and hyperbolic functions. Built-in constants include pi, e and sample_rate. Definitions can refer to previously declared controls. Source: docs/eff_guide.html
