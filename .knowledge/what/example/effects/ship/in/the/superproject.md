effects/ contains eighteen library descriptors plus older examples. The foundational set is LEVEL, INVPHASE, CLIP, CUBEDRV, OCTAVE, SVFLP/SVFHP/SVFBP/SVFNOTCH, SVFLP4 and SVFTONE. Further experiments are WAH, VOWEL, OCTFUZZ, DRIVE, GROWL, BASSRING and FLANGE. They use arithmetic, private-state Chamberlin SVF, polynomial, scratchpad state and integer delays, never the old general filter engine. All filenames fit carrier 8.3 FAT; effects/README.md owns controls/public use.

FLANGE uses a nominal 0.1–3-Hz oscillator to move a four-sample base tap through 0.2–8-ms depth. Two scratchpad words retain phase using ordinary arithmetic. An odd degree-seven polynomial approximates sin(pi*x) for x in [-1,1]; coefficients are {0,3.1401887343,0,-5.1363218456,0,2.4271414428,0,-0.4310083315}. It replaces the sine-ROM instruction and deliberately exercises the polynomial engine. All 65,536 signed16 inputs match model/RTL, with maximum analytic error 23.642 codes and wrap endpoints 0/3. The actual shape check takes at most 37 cycles/sample. tools/test_eff_flange.py retains that independent sine/wrap check.

Feedback spans 0–0.85, with input writes scaled by 1−feedback. Output weights balance the comb response while retaining unity DC: at Mix=0.5, dry/wet weights are (1−feedback)/2 and (1+feedback)/2. Independent fixed-tap checks have notch residuals 0/31/43 codes at feedback 0/0.65/0.85, exceeding 40 dB suppression across 49,152 outputs. David prefers this quieter, watery version over the energy-normalized variant, so the original weights are restored exactly. Do not automatically substitute a loudness-normalization target for his sonic preference. The declared 512-word delay becomes 516 after padding, with startup muting/fade and no fractional interpolation. FLANGE remains 27 blocks and at most 316 cycles/sample.

Current default/corner verification checks 106 programs and 1,514,696 exact outputs: 74 unchanged programs in /tmp/kestrel-effect-library/results.json plus five each for the revised WAH/VOWEL in /tmp/kestrel-wah-bass-body/results.json and /tmp/kestrel-vowel-bass-formants/results.json, five current polynomial ring programs in /tmp/kestrel-bassring-poly/results.json and seventeen current polynomial FLANGE programs in /tmp/kestrel-flange-poly/results.json (175,172 outputs). Selected response/identity/polarity/clipping checks pass. GROWL, BASSRING and FLANGE preserve dry input exactly at Mix=0. Default costs span 18–316 cycles/sample. This is simulation evidence, not blanket sonic acceptance. Earlier full-period tests qualify the unchanged phase accumulator at nominal 0.1/0.3/3 Hz with measured 0.105143/0.304914/2.996535 Hz; the older complete sustained renders used the ROM waveform, so they do not replace current polynomial waveform qualification.

BASSRING multiplies input by a free-running 20–220-Hz polynomial sine carrier with persistent phase. Full-wet Mix is the default; the mix is compensated by 1/sqrt((1−mix)^2+mix^2/2), giving nominal equal RMS rather than the sine product's inherent attenuation. It remains signal-dependent and can saturate loud inputs. Q15 frequency steps are roughly 1.35 Hz. The revised source has 15 blocks and takes at most 182 cycles/sample. All five default/corner programs pass exact RTL comparison.

GROWL multiplies driven 400-Hz low-pass and 1200-Hz band-pass components and uses the low component for sample-by-sample cutoff motion of a third SVF. David hears HPF/distortion with nonmusical gain changes. The earlier WAH/VOWEL were barely noticeable and cut away bass fundamentals. Current versions retain dry input and add normalized resonant bands at 2/Q instead: WAH is a resonant peak, and VOWEL adds two moving formants at 100–500 Hz and 1400–400 Hz as Mouth goes from 0 to 1. David found the earlier dry-preserving, higher-frequency revision approximately the identity. The current lower centers produce a substantial change on synthetic bass, but David still hears an ultra-mild result and cannot identify a meaningful transformation. This version also fails the requested character. At fixed Mouth it is simply EQ: dry signal plus two resonant peaks, with manual movement only; 'Talking Vowel' overstates the demonstrated sound. WAH listening acceptance remains open. OCTFUZZ/DRIVE are distortion flavors. Those earlier auditions have not satisfied his request for interesting musical transformations. David plays bass exclusively and wants arbitrary audio-rate DSP exploited, including cross-modulation, dynamic filters, stateful/nonlinear feedback and time/modulation effects.
