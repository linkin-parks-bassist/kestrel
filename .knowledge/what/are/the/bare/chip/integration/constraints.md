Use GW2AR-18 QN88 deliberately; packages are not interchangeable. Gowin specifies 3.3 V on SDRAM-connected banks 2/3/6/7, with JTAG and MSPI configuration. Preserve configuration flash and service JTAG. Core VCC and filtered VCCPLL require 0.95–1.05 V. QN88 shared VCCX/SDRAM I/O operating range is 3.135–3.6 V; Rev-B allocates 3.135–3.465 V for its nominal 3.3-V domain.

UG206-1.9.3E gives a 0.1–10-ms power-on reference range, without a special VCC sequence within that reference. If power-on exceeds 10 ms, VCC must precede VCCX/VCCIO. VCCX must always be at least VCCIO; raise it before or with I/O rails to avoid unexpected bank current. Main TPS54302 nominal 5-ms soft start alone does not trigger the greater-than-10-ms condition. Rails must rise monotonically; ramp rates are VCC 0.1–10 mV/us and VCCX/VCCIO 0.01–100 mV/us. Configuration waits until required rails are in range. Rev-B feeds fixed core independently from 5 V and qualifies/gates FPGA 3.3 V after core stabilization. Implemented circuit and conditional checks belong to what/is/the/fpga/power/circuit.md; measurements remain. Provide per-pin 100-nF decoupling and PLL isolation. Sources: https://cdn.gowinsemi.com.cn/UG206E.pdf (UG206-1.9.3E), https://cdn.gowinsemi.com.cn/UG115E.pdf (UG115-1.7.2E); carrier reference https://dl.sipeed.com/fileList/TANG/Nano_20K/2_Schematic/Tang_Nano_20K_3921_Schematics.pdf. QN88 pin/footprint evidence and instantiated support circuit belong to what/is/the/bare/fpga/support/circuit.md. Exact parts, current consumption, domain behavior and stencil/layout qualification remain.

For the MCU target use ESP32-P4NRW16X v3.x, with NRW32X a memory option. Espressif PCN202600801 recommends these replacements and plans EOL for NRW16/NRW32. Pin 54 is VDD_HP_1. Populate both 499-kOhm HP feedback resistors and the 22-pF feed-forward capacitor; the old USB_DP 1-MOhm resistor is removed. HP power is separately regulated and controlled through EN_DCDC/FB_DCDC; never tie it to FPGA fixed core. Current guidance specifies a 40-MHz crystal, external firmware flash, in-package PSRAM and 10-kOhm/1-uF reset RC starting point. Follow the current chip-specific reference circuit. Sources: https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32p4/schematic-checklist-esp32p4.html and https://documentation.espressif.com/PCN202600801_ESP32-P4_Chip_Revision_v3.2_Upgrade_Chip_Revision_v1.3_Demand_Collection_and_EOL_Plan_Description.pdf.

P4 v3.2 requires ESP-IDF v5.5.3+ or v6.0+, ESP32P4_SELECTS_REV_LESS_V3=n and a compatible esptool. Previous and new revisions cannot share one binary.
