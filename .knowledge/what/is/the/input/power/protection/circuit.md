Rev-B uses a nominal regulated centre-negative 9-V pedal supply through an onboard PJ-102AH jack, resettable PTC and P-channel MOSFET, with a transient clamp and local ceramic bypass. `kestrel_pcb/rev-b/tools/build_input_power.py` generates `electrical/kestrel-revb-input-power.kicad_sch` and the portable AO4407A symbol. Run power, audio, FPGA power, input power, then project generators. This engineering draft supplies the shared 9V_PROTECTED net; it is not a qualified system protection rating.

J401 [Same Sky PJ-102AH](https://www.sameskydevices.com/product/resource/pj-102ah.pdf) is through-hole, right-angle, with 2.0-mm centre pin and 5.5-mm mating barrel drawing. Pin1 is centre/GND; pin2 is sleeve/positive; pin3 is the unused sleeve-normal contact, NC. Its manufacturer drawing agrees with KiCad BarrelJack_CUI_PJ-102AH_Horizontal pad spacing: pins1/2 separated by 6 mm; pin3 offset 4.7 mm sideways and 3 mm midway. Stock 1.6-mm circular drills replace the drawing's 1x1.6-mm slots; lead-fit/retention and mating-plug/case datums need assembly checks. Vendor rates the jack 5 A/24 V; this is not board capacity. Underside through-hole post assembly is the working power-jack placement; audio jacks also use underside assembly. The current manufacturer drawing gives an 11 mm body height above its contact plane. At PCB top Z=23.6 mm, a top-side body reaches Z=34.6 mm and overlaps the reference panel bottom Z=32.62 mm by 1.98 mm wherever their XY envelopes overlap. Moving the jack below PCB bottom Z=22 mm gives nominal body bottom Z=11 mm; actual case/plug access, tolerances and lead clearance remain unqualified. Primary drawing: https://www.sameskydevices.com/product/resource/pj-102ah.pdf, stored as mechanical/vendor/same-sky/pj-102ah.pdf. Its current mating insertion depth is 9.5 mm; this does not establish reach through the rear casting wall.

F401 [Littelfuse 1812L200/16DR](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C439873.html) is a 16-V 1812 resettable PTC, 2-A hold/3.5-A trip at 20 C. Its [manufacturer datasheet](https://www.littelfuse.com/~/media/electronics/datasheets/resettable_ptcs/littelfuse_ptc_1812l_datasheet.pdf.pdf) gives R1max 0.070 ohm after trip/reflow at 20 C and 2-s maximum trip example at 8 A. That timing does not apply at 3.5 A; hot hold-current derating and fault coordination remain to qualify against the actual load/source.

Q401 [AOS AO4407A](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AO4407A.pdf) uses source pins1/2/3 on 9V_PROTECTED, gate pin4 pulled to ground through R401 100k/1%, and drain pins5/6/7/8 on fused jack input. The body diode points input drain to output source, establishing startup bias before the channel turns on. Reversing this orientation would defeat reverse-polarity protection. The portable symbol copies KiCad IRF7404's PMOS drawing/stacked pins with corrected identity/datasheet and checked package pinout. AO4407A is rated 30-V VDS and ±25-V VGS; manufacturer maximum RDS(on) at -6-V VGS is 17 milliohms under its room-temperature test conditions. At an assumed 2-A input, conditional FET/PTC drops are 0.034/0.140 V and losses 0.068/0.280 W. The 9-V ±5% allocation leaves 8.376 V before jack/copper losses. These are not thermal or all-temperature bounds. A single PMOS does not block output-to-input current when other sources power the board; USB/JTAG/other-domain backfeed still needs resolution.

D401 is unidirectional [Littelfuse SMAJ9.0A](https://www.littelfuse.com/~/media/electronics/datasheets/tvs_diodes/littelfuse_tvs_diode_smaj_datasheet.pdf.pdf), cathode pad1 to protected 9 V, anode pad2 to ground. It uses a K/A zener symbol rather than the bidirectional Device:D_TVS symbol. Manufacturer table gives 9-V standoff, 10–11.1-V breakdown at 1 mA and 15.4-V clamp at 26 A/10–1000-us/25 C. LCSC's product attributes conflict with this exact MPN by showing 10-V/17-V data; use manufacturer data. The pulse table alone does not establish overshoot, continuous overvoltage tolerance or fuse coordination. Place clamp/bypass at entry with a short jack-ground return; qualify temperature, leakage above nominal input and waveform/layout effects.
