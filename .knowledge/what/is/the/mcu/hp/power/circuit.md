Rev-B instantiates the separately controlled ESP32-P4 v3 HP supply in kestrel_pcb/rev-b/electrical/kestrel-revb-mcu-power.kicad_sch, generated by tools/build_mcu_power.py and linked by build_project.py. This is an engineering draft; the bare MCU is connected in the schematic, while hardware qualification remains.

U601 TLV62569DBVR uses SOT-23-5: pin1 EN to MCU_EN_DCDC, pin2 ground, pin3 switch through L601 to ESP_VDD_HP, pin4 VIN from main +3V3_D, pin5 FB to MCU_FB_DCDC. Those controls connect to U701 P4 pins79/78. HP output feeds P4 pins26/54/76/91 with individual local bypass and bulk; it is separate from the fixed FPGA core. tools/check_mcu_power.py verifies these integrated connections.

R601/R602 are populated 499-kOhm 1% resistors from HP output to FB and FB to ground; C605 is 22-pF 25-V C0G across the upper resistor. This follows Espressif's v3 reference circuit. Both control pins are internally controlled by P4; EN is low in download/no-firmware mode. Equal resistors alone would nominally set 1.2 V at a 0.6-V regulator reference, but do not model actual P4-controlled operating voltage. Source: https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32p4/schematic-checklist-esp32p4.html, current v3 TLV62569 image.

L601 is selected Sunlord MWSA0402S-2R2MT, 2.2 uH, C408335, with Inductor_SMD:L_Sunlord_MWSA0402S. The primary catalog lists 4 A at 20% inductance loss and 5 A at 30% loss, at 20 C ambient, maximum DCR 58 milliohm. Recommended 1.5×2.5 mm lands with 2.2 mm gap match KiCad centres ±1.85 mm; maximum body height is2 mm. These cover the preliminary 3.5 A current allocation only at that catalog condition. Actual load, temperature, fault and Sydney sourcing qualification remain. Catalog/current-criterion explanation and selection-field generator belong to what/is/the/integrated/power/circuit.md. C601 is 4.7-uF 16-V X7R input, C602 100-nF; C603/C604 are two 22-uF 16-V X7R output capacitors. Two output capacitors cover TI's recommended 2.2-uH LC combination below 1.2 V while accommodating the stated P4 HP range 0.99–1.30 V. Effective capacitance, saturation/DC resistance, actual load, transient stability and controlled feedback loop must be checked. Source: TI TLV62569 SLVSDG1C pin table and Table4, https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv62569.pdf. Espressif's standalone reference image shows only one 22-uF output; the extra capacitor is an engineering selection based on TI's lower-voltage recommendation.

Regulator 3.3 V input, via-annulus/process qualification, feedback noise/stability and silicon-controlled startup/brownout remain unresolved. Ground continuity does not qualify stackup, return impedance, load/current/thermal or complete-board routing. Trace widths and this connectivity check do not establish current capacity, thermal performance, loop stability or operation.

The HP PWR_FLAG describes the regulator source, not voltage validity. Verify source-current budget, placement beside MCU, feedback routing, boot/sleep/brownout behavior and 0.99–1.30-V operation before declaring the supply functional.
