Rev-B has a fixed FPGA core regulator and a supervised, switched 3.3-V auxiliary/I/O supply in `kestrel_pcb/rev-b/electrical/kestrel-revb-fpga-power.kicad_sch`. `tools/build_fpga_power.py` generates the sheet, portable symbols and load-switch footprint; run after the audio generator, before `build_project.py`. This is an engineering draft, not hardware validation. Keep FPGA core separate from the ESP32-P4 variable HP supply.

U301 TLV62569DBVR takes +5V_DIG, with EN tied to that input. L301 is selected Sunlord MWSA0402S-2R2MT, 2.2 uH, C408335, with Inductor_SMD:L_Sunlord_MWSA0402S. Sunlord lists 4 A at 20% inductance loss and 5 A at 30% loss at 20 C ambient, maximum DCR 58 milliohm; full-temperature/fault and actual load/thermal qualification remain. Nominal body 4.4×4.2 mm, height max2 mm; recommended 1.5×2.5 mm pads with 2.2 mm gap match KiCad centres ±1.85 mm. Primary catalog, field-generation procedure and current-criterion explanation belong to what/is/the/integrated/power/circuit.md. C301 is 10-uF/16-V X7R, C302/C303 two 22-uF/16-V X7R output capacitors. R301/R302 68.1k/100k, 0.1%, and parallel 6.8-pF C0G feedforward give nominal 1.0086 V. [TI TLV62569](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv62569.pdf) recommends this LC starting combination for 0.6–1.2-V output. The DBV package has no power-good pin. Conditional DC corners are 0.9876–1.0296 V using TI VFB limits specified at VIN=5 V/TJ=25 C; ripple, line/load, temperature, current capacity and stability remain unverified. The 2-A IC rating is not established board capacity.

[Gowin UG206-1.9.3E](https://cdn.gowinsemi.com.cn/UG206E.pdf) requires VCCX always at least VCCIO and recommends VCCX before/with VCCIO. Its power-on reference is 0.1–10 ms; core-first ordering is required when power-on exceeds 10 ms, rather than for the main buck's nominal 5-ms soft start. The chosen circuit qualifies core before releasing a common switched VCCX/I/O domain. Qualification delay between rails is distinct from each rail's rise time. [UG115-1.7.2E](https://cdn.gowinsemi.com.cn/UG115E.pdf) gives core/PLL operating range 0.95–1.05 V and shared QN88 VCCX/SDRAM I/O 3.135–3.6 V; Rev-B uses the tighter 3.135–3.465-V rail allocation. Check monotonic ramps, VCC 0.1–10 mV/us, VCCX/VCCIO 0.01–100 mV/us and configuration after all required rails are in range on hardware. U302 TPS3897ADRYR monitors core with 9.31k/10k, 0.1%, plus 1-nF filter; VCC/ENABLE use main +3V3_D. Its active-high open-drain output drives FPGA_IO_EN. [TI TPS3897A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3895.pdf) has 0.495–0.505-V positive-going threshold over temperature and ±15-nA sense-current limits. Conditional divider release is 0.9548–0.9762 V, below the calculated regulator minimum by 11.4 mV. This margin needs ripple/transient validation. C311 4.7-nF/5%/25-V C0G sets nominal 18.84-ms qualification delay. External CT charge calculation gives 14.64–24.66 ms using threshold/current/capacitor limits; it is not a guaranteed total propagation-delay interval. The checker enforces a chosen 10-ms minimum external CT charge allocation, not a vendor rail-rise limit or proof that core is settled. Hysteresis is 5 mV typical without specified min/max: falling brownout cutoff above 0.95 V is not guaranteed.

Equal 10k, 1% pullup to +3V3_D and pulldown on FPGA_IO_EN provide approximately 1.65-V high. With the 3.135–3.465-V allocation and leakage allowance, calculated minimum is 1.548 V, above TPS22965 ON VIH=1.1 V. Below supervisor's 0.8-V POR level, the divided-input allocation stays below ON VIL=0.5 V; verify dynamic startup/shutdown and leakage conditions on hardware.

U303 [TPS22965DSGR](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22965.pdf) gates +3V3_D to +3V3_FPGA. VIN pins 1/2 use main 3.3 V, ON pin3 uses FPGA_IO_EN, VBIAS pin4 uses +5V_DIG, GND pin5/EP9 ground, CT pin6 has 470-pF/25-V C0G, and VOUT pins7/8 use the switched rail. CT must tolerate its boosted voltage; 25-V rating is intentional. Starting bypass is 10 uF input, 100 nF bias and 4.7 uF output. TI's 470-pF/3.3-V/5-V-bias example rise is 654 us typical, not a timing bound. Internal quick-output discharge is present. Custom DSG lands use TI's 0.5x0.25-mm pads at x=±0.95 mm, pitch 0.5 mm, with 0.9x1.6-mm ground EP. KiCad body/courtyard/model/paste are retained; stencil review remains. TPS3897 uses stock Texas USON-6 DRY footprint with solder-mask-defined lands.
