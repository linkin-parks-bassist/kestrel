Rev-B connects two active-low momentary footswitch channels on `kestrel_pcb/rev-b/electrical/kestrel-revb-footswitch.kicad_sch`, generated by `tools/build_footswitch.py`. Left uses P4 GPIO20/QFN pad22 and right GPIO21/pad23, preserving existing Interface driver GPIOs. Panel-mounted normally-open contacts connect through two wires to J1101/J1102: 1 signal, 2 ground. J1101/J1102 use the provisionally selected stock `Connector_Wire:SolderWire-0.127sqmm_1x02_P3.7mm_D0.48mm_OD1mm_Relief2x`: 3.7-mm electrical pitch, 0.7-mm electrical drills and four 1.5-mm non-plated strain-relief drills at 6/12-mm offsets. The schematic generator, child sheet, exported XML and PCB agree. `electrical/footswitch-support-placement.json` and `tools/place_footswitch_support.py` place all fourteen wire/ESD/RC/pullup/damping/bypass support parts on F.Cu. J1101 is at KiCad (75,134), rotation 0; J1102 at (154.7,126), rotation 90, clearing the underside output-jack body and nearby mounting hole. U1101/U1102 retain (78,131)/(122,131). The guarded placer refuses manually changed poses, supports trial output, preserves footprint/pad UUIDs, hierarchy and nets, and refills the existing plane. `generated/footswitch-support-placement-review.json` checks saved poses/drills, all prior copper, unrelated footprint/pad geometry and previous report inventories. Native copper/mask/hole/courtyard/edge checks and import/topology/analogue continuity checks pass; `generated/pcb-footswitch-placement.svg`/PNG show native review. Wire conductor/insulation selection, bends, strain-relief threading, solder access, physical clearance and assembly remain unqualified. SW1101/SW1102 are included as off-board assembly parts with `on_board no`/no footprint, so foot loads belong on the enclosure.

Each raw wire has a PESD5V0U1BA,115 bipolar ground shunt (D1101/1102), then 1 kΩ series (R1101/1104) to its filtered node. R1102/1105 pull that node to main +3V3_D through 10 kΩ. C1101/1103 are 100 nF/16 V X7R with required 80–120 nF effective range. U1101/U1102 SN74LVC1G17DCKR Schmitt buffers use NC1, input2, GND3, output4 and VCC5; each has local 100 nF bypass C1102/1104. Outputs reach MCU through R1103/1106 33 Ω starting source damping. All resistors are 1%; input electronics are SMD.

`electrical/footswitch-local-routes.json` and `tools/build_footswitch_local.py` implement all eight board-side signal nets, the six-pad main-3.3-V footswitch load branch and all ten footswitch ground returns. It owns 118 segments (twenty F.Cu, 85 In2.Cu, thirteen B.Cu), 37 0.5/0.3-mm through vias, 39 guarded pad anchors and 26 named via-in-pad sites. Local RC/Schmitt/bypass paths use provisional 0.254-mm copper, supply trunks 0.508 mm and MCU signal/escape paths 0.127 mm. The main feed branches from the existing 0.8/0.3-mm +3V3_D via at (65.45,118.8), also on the ADC digital feed; its identity/geometry is guarded and source-to-load continuity is checked back to L102. Both QFN escapes clear existing HP/main-power and SPI copper, using outside-land terminal vias at (116.5,89.2) and (115.85,89.7). `tools/check_footswitch_local.py` checks stable serialized geometry/report ownership, native named-drill containment, exact board/XML membership of all eight signal nets (excluding the two declared panel switches), all owned via reachability, the supply subset reaching L102 and all ten actual footswitch grounds reaching filled In1.Cu. With main rail allocation 3.2060–3.3826 V and assumed total input/board leakage ≤10 µA, inactive input is at least 3.105 V and pressed input at most about 0.322 V. Contact current is approximately 0.289–0.311 mA. Charging RC is 0.792–1.212 ms; pressed discharge RC is 72–110 µs. These are conditional calculations, not complete contact debounce. Comparisons to TI's 3 V/4.5 V tabulated threshold extremes (negative minimum 0.89 V, positive maximum 2.74 V) do not establish a guaranteed interpolation across the actual rail. Threshold/temperature, leakage and effective capacitance need qualification. [TI buffer primary datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g17.pdf).
