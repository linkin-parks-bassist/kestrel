Rev-B implements the reference DM-TFT50-404 RGB565/capacitive-touch interface in `kestrel_pcb/rev-b/electrical/kestrel-revb-display.kicad_sch`, generated by `tools/build_display.py`. The shared A2 backlight sheet supplies LCD and touch power. This is connected engineering circuitry, not a qualified purchasing choice or proven display.

U903 TLV76701DRVR produces LCD_VDD from +5V_DIG: OUT 1, FB 2, GND 3/5/EP7, EN4 and IN6. R906/R907 are 33.2 kΩ/10 kΩ, 0.1%; C905/C906 4.7 µF/16 V X7R, 0805, each requires ≥1 µF effective; C907 10 pF/25 V C0G spans upper feedback. TP903 probes output. Nominal 3.456 V has conditional DC bounds 3.414525–3.497592 V, using ±1% reference, ±0.1% divider and conservative ±50 nA bias. It fits the panel's literal 3.3–3.6 V supply requirement assuming regulation/source headroom.

U904 uses the same pin topology with R908/R909 30 kΩ/10 kΩ, 0.1%, C908/C909 4.7 µF/16 V X7R with ≥1 µF effective, C910 10 pF C0G and TP904. Nominal TOUCH_VDD is 3.2 V; conditional bounds 3.161751–3.238354 V. It supplies both touch VDDIO and VDD. Goodix Rev.09 recommends AVDD 2.8–3.3 V and VDDIO 1.8–3.3 V. The panel brief's 2.66–3.47 V matches absolute limits, not normal operating limits. Neither main buck's 3.3826 V upper allocation nor LCD_VDD is the touch supply. Maximum loads, source headroom, ceramic bias, thermal and rail ramps remain unqualified.

J1001 is 40-pin/0.5 mm LCD: 1 LED cathode/current-sense return, 2 anode, 3/29/36 ground, 4 LCD_VDD, 5–12 R0–7, 13–20 G0–7, 21–28 B0–7, 30 PCLK, 31 DISP, 32 HSYNC, 33 VSYNC, 34 DE, 35/37–40 NC. RGB565 uses B3–7, G2–7, R3–7; lower unused bits are grounded. J1002 is 8-pin/0.5 mm touch: 1/8 ground, 2 VDDIO and 3 VDD on TOUCH_VDD, 4 SCL, 5 SDA, 6 INT, 7 active-low reset. Assigned Hirose FH12-40S/8S-0.5SH(55) bottom-contact footprints are provisional: verify exposed flex side, pin numbering, 0.3 mm tail thickness and bend/retention geometry before placement/fabrication. Primary drawing distinguishes component and contact sides but does not prove assembled fit.

GPIO9 PCLK, 10 HSYNC, 11 VSYNC, 12 DE, 13 DISP; GPIO15 SCL, 16 SDA, 17 INT, 18 reset. P4 GPIO-matrix routing permits this allocation without fixed RGB IOMUX pins. Existing flash, boot, service, SPI and PWM pins are preserved. R1001–R1025 are 33 Ω source-damping starting values. R1026/R1027 are 4.7 kΩ I2C pullups to TOUCH_VDD; R1028/R1029 are 10 kΩ DISP/reset pulldowns; R1030 is 100 kΩ INT pulldown. C1001–C1004 are 100 nF connector supply bypasses. INT must be bidirectional during address/reset initialization; Goodix wire addresses BA/BB and 28/29 correspond to 7-bit 5D and 14.
