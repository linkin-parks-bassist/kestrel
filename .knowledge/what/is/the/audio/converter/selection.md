The integrated revision replaces SGTL5000 with hardware-configured PCM1808PWR ADC plus PCM5102APWR DAC, subject to supply/clock/analogue validation and a Sydney delivery quote. Mono external I/O remains the requirement; stereo silicon does not change it.

David requires the converters to remain audio slaves and the FPGA to drive MCLK, as in the carrier. The FPGA also owns BCLK and LRCLK. [PCM1808](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/pcm1808.pdf) needs synchronous MCLK and is strapped FMT/MD1/MD0 low for 24-bit I2S slave operation. It has nominal 99 dB dynamic range and 3 Vpp input, with 5 V analogue and 3.3 V digital supplies. The draft includes an OPA1656 input buffer, independent bias buffer, approximate half-scale attenuation, AC coupling and input RC filtering; these need tolerance, protection and bench verification.

[PCM5102A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/pcm5102a.pdf) accepts FPGA BCLK/LRCLK/data, with SCK grounded to use its internal BCLK PLL. This preserves slave operation without a separate DAC MCLK connection. The draft selects its low-latency filter and includes 3.3 V power, charge-pump/output filtering and default-low mute. Its outputs are ground-centred, nominally 2.1 Vrms. Jack ESD/input steering and supervised mute/isolated supply are in the draft. Protection, hold-up, GPIO sequencing and startup/shutdown still require validation; mute details belong to `what/is/the/audio/mute/circuit.md`. Neither converter requires I2C register setup.
