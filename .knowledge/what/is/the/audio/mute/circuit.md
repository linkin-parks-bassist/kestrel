D204 SS14-E3/61T feeds +5V_DAC_HOLD from buck +5V_DIG. Parallel C230/C235 are Panasonic EEEFK1A102P, each 1000 uF, 10 V, ±20%. U205 supplies held +3V3_A. DAC AVDD, CPVDD, DVDD, FLT, both supervisors and U504's AUDIO_ENABLE buffer share it with separate bypass; main digital loads stay outside this domain.

U206 monitors ADC +5V_A and U207 buck +5V_DIG. Each has a 105k/10k 0.1% divider and 1-nF filter, nominal falling threshold 4.6575 V. Open-drain resets wire together as AUDIO_RUN/DAC XSMT, pulled up 10k to held 3.3 V and down 100k. Both MR pins connect to AUDIO_ENABLE with 1k pulldown. Held-supply U504 SN74LVC1G17DCKR actively buffers FPGA pin20 into this net; its FPGA-side input has 10k pulldown and no direct connection to MR pullups. The implementation and power-domain limitations are owned by `what/is/the/bare/fpga/support/circuit.md`. CT pins are NC for 12–28 ms release delay, nominal 20 ms. FPGA must keep enable low through configuration/startup, assert it only after clocks/data and supplies stabilize, and mute before deliberate shutdown. GPIO selection, levels/current and implementation remain to verify.

[Panasonic FK datasheet](https://industrial.panasonic.com/cdbs/www-data/pdf/RDE0000/ABA0000C1181.pdf) and [part specification](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/aluminum-cap-smd/models/EEEFK1A102P) specify 10-mm diameter, 10.2±0.3-mm height, 10.3±0.2-mm base, 2000 h at 105 C, 80-mohm maximum impedance at 100 kHz and 100-uA leakage at rated voltage after two minutes at 20 C. Neither is a full-temperature transient ESR/leakage guarantee. Endurance allows ±30% capacitance change from initial, measured after stabilization at 20 C. Local footprint `KestrelAudio:CP_Elec_Panasonic_FK_G_10x10.2` uses recommended size-G standard lands: 4.6-mm gap, two 4.1x2.0-mm rectangular pads centred ±4.35 mm. Pin1 is positive. It loads in KiCad 10; body/courtyard and approximate 3D model derive from KiCad's generic 10x10.5-mm capacitor. `fp-lib-table` resolves it relative to the project.

[TI PCM5102A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/pcm5102a.pdf) needs 150/fs plus 0.2 ms to mute, 3.325 ms at 48 kHz. `tools/check_audio_mute.py` checks netlist identity, parallel capacitor polarity and mute isolation, and writes `generated/audio-mute-review.json`. Base calculation gives 11.001 ms hold-up, with assumptions 1600-uF minimum, 65-mA total held load, including an added 5-mA buffer/MR allocation, 0.5-V diode drop, 0.1-ohm combined reservoir impedance and 3.6-V LDO input minimum. The aged room-temperature case uses 1120 uF and gives 7.700 ms. Extra sensitivity allocation of 1008 uF, 70 mA, 0.6-V drop and 0.4-ohm total impedance gives 4.686 ms, or 1.261-ms margin after a 100-us detection allowance. Two capacitors avoid the fragile aged margin of a single one. These allocations still need verification across temperature/reflow/life; TI specifies typical sense response, not a guaranteed maximum for the assumed allowance. No hardware hold-up or pop-free rating is established.

[TPS3808](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808.pdf) threshold corners give 4.553–4.762 V falling and conservative 4.905-V maximum release. Calculated MR default-low is 0.0935 V with both minimum-70k internal pullups. [Vishay SS14](https://www.vishay.com/docs/88746/ss12.pdf) gives diode limits. Reverse discharge/leakage, actual load, regulator dropout/discharge and buck inrush remain to check.
