U203 dual OPA1656IDR SOIC-8 implements input and independent bias followers, with 47-ohm isolation before bias decoupling. 17.4k/10k gives nominal 3.334 V at 9 V. Input 47-nF C0G coupling, 10M pulldown, 1M bias and 4.7k series resistance yield approximately 0.91 Mohm impedance and 3.4 Hz coupling corner. The 1k/1k divider approximately halves AC before ADC coupling. ADC VREF is not an op-amp bias source. Headroom, bias settling, stability and noise need verification.

J201/J202 are NMJ4HCD2 switched mono through-hole sockets using matching AudioJack2_Switch symbols/footprints, pads T/TN/S/SN. D201/D203 are [PESD5V0U1BA,115](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/PESD5V0U1BA.pdf) bipolar ESD shunts from each jack tip to ground, preserving negative audio swing. D202 [BAV199,215](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/BAV199.pdf) steers IN_BIAS to ground/protected 9 V: pin1 anode ground, pin2 cathode rail, pin3 junction input. Its custom symbol copies the stock series-diode drawing but corrects names, identity and datasheet against Nexperia. It has a specified 5-nA maximum reverse leakage per diode at 25 C/75 V, suitable for high-impedance bias compared with leaky clamps. R208 is 4.7k, 1%, 1206 with at least 0.25 W and pulse rating pending; C221 requires 50 V C0G. Conditional 24 V across R208 gives at most 5.158 mA/0.124 W at minus-1% resistance, below OPA1656's 10-mA input-current recommendation. This does not prove a 24-V jack fault rating. R208's calculated Johnson noise alone at 300 K/20 kHz is 1.248 uVrms before attenuation. TVS transient residuals, resistor pulses, rail backfeed when unpowered, sustained faults and output-driver protection require layout/bench verification; no system ESD rating is established.
